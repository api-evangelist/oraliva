Oral cancer is one of the few cancers dentists see coming and still, too often, catch too late. Most cases are visible during a routine exam, yet nearly 3 in 4 aren’t identified until a late stage, when five-year survival drops from roughly 88% to just 37%. The problem isn’t awareness.
